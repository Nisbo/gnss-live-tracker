# Troubleshooting · GNSS Live Tracker V2

The practical checklists are in [English installation](INSTALL.en.md#troubleshooting-and-recovery) and [deutscher Anleitung](INSTALL.de.md#fehlerhilfe-und-wiederherstellung-per-usb).

For a support report include board revision, firmware version, selected region/rate/ADR, whether GNSS has a fix, which recording destination is selected, and the relevant redacted error. The Diagnostics tab shows uptime/reset cause and memory. A locally completed LoRaWAN transmission does not prove TTN reception.

Do not attach backups, private keys, Wi-Fi passwords or unredacted positions. A full reinstall is destructive; it is not the first troubleshooting step. See [recovery instructions](BUILD.md#command-line-usb-recovery-destructive).
